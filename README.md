# 🚨 Women Safety Analytics - Protecting Women from Safety Threats

The growing concern for women's safety and increasing incidents of crimes in various cities highlight the urgent need for advanced surveillance and analytical solutions.

This project aims to enhance **women's safety** using **AI-powered analytics** and mobile-based surveillance.

---

## 📱 Solution Overview

Our AI-based Women Safety Analytics system utilizes the phone camera to **analyze the surrounding environment** and detect the **number of males and females** present nearby.

It identifies potentially risky situations such as:
- A lone woman surrounded by multiple men.
- A woman being alone in unsafe hours (e.g., late night).

In such scenarios, the system **automatically triggers an SOS pop-up** on the user’s phone, enabling her to quickly send an alert.

**Key Features:**
- 🚻 **Real-Time Gender Detection:** Detects and counts males & females around the user.
- ⚠️ **Risk Detection & Auto-Alert:** Recognizes risky situations and auto-triggers SOS alerts.
- 🆘 **SOS Pop-up & Quick Response:** Instant SOS message pop-up for emergency use.
- 📍 **Location Sharing:** Sends real-time location and situation details to nearby police stations or emergency services.

---

## 💡 How It Works
1. 📷 The phone camera analyzes surroundings using AI models.
2. 🧠 The system processes video frames to detect genders and counts individuals.
3. 🚨 Risky situations trigger an **automated SOS pop-up** on the phone.
4. 📨 User can confirm the alert to send their **live location** & situation details to emergency contacts or authorities.
